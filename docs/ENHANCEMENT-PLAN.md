## 1. Baseline — what's already shipped (the launchpad)

The platform is further along than the roadmap's checkbox state suggests:

- ✅ **Booking M1–M4 complete** (both adapters): request → respond (quote ± deposit) → deposit checkout/webhook → reminder cron → reschedule → cancel with the 24h refund-policy window → inProgress/completed/noShow transitions; customer + worker UIs, `/bookings` lookup, admin funnel + dispute view, full audit + notification trail. See [booking-scheduling.md](booking-scheduling.md) and [selection-workflow.md](selection-workflow.md).
- ✅ **Payments infrastructure** (simulated/Stripe-gated): deposit Payment rows, idempotent checkout minting, webhook confirm, provider refunds, `WA-YYYY-NNNNN` invoices for signed-in customers, campaign purchase path (create → checkout → ACTIVE) with admin refunds + credit-note voiding + `campaignRefunded` email, all Prisma-backed in real mode. The admin campaign **Refund button is now a two-step confirm** (`RefundDialog` in `src/components/dashboard/refund-dialog.tsx`, extracted from the admin dashboard): step 1 collects the refund reason (Continue disabled until non-empty), step 2 shows an irreversible-action summary — **campaign name, amount (formatted), and the exact reason** — with Back (preserves the reason, no action) and a single "Confirm refund · $amount" commit button that fires `refundCampaignAction`; Cancel/Esc/overlay discard with no side effects, and the dialog locks while the provider refund is in flight. Mirrors the plan-change confirm pattern so an irreversible money action needs a deliberate commit. i18n `admin.refund*` keys EN+AR; component tests in `tests/refund-dialog.test.tsx` (two-step happy path, Back-preserves-reason, cancel-never-fires).
- ✅ **Monetization foundations**: worker subscriptions (incl. annual plans), self-serve ads (PENDING→ACTIVE gate), admin revenue cards (net-collected campaign totals), booking-funnel admin card.
- ✅ **Lebanon launch — OMT & Whish manual payments + the revenue quick wins (BUSINESS-MODEL §5.1, no Stripe)** — Lebanon is a first-class service country (Beirut in the CITIES catalog, LBP currency); the OMT/Whish providers mint a **signed `/payments/manual` instructions page** (per-provider salt + `OMT-`/`WHISH-` ref prefixes, verified via each provider's own `verifyWebhook`) and an **admin confirms receipt** from the `/admin` pending-payments card (`getPendingManualPayments` + `confirmManualPaymentAction` — the manual twin of a webhook, idempotent, admin-only). The method threads through every money flow (`payBookingAction` / `payCampaignAction` / `renewSubscriptionAction` / `purchaseUpgradeAction`, stamped on the Payment row; checkout minting is idempotent **per method** — a method switch re-mints; refunds route through the paying provider). Paid upgrades are purchasable via OMT/Whish and admin-confirmed: **verification tiers** (Basic $9 / Professional $19 → `verified` badge), **Featured slot** ($49/category/mo) and the **Emergency marker** ($9/mo) — worker dashboard upgrade dialog + method picker, EN/AR i18n, tests in `tests/lebanon-payments.test.ts` (17: providers/registry round-trip, booking + campaign deposits, renewal, upgrades, the manual-queue lifecycle) + `tests/manual-payments-card.test.tsx` (5). Full docs in [PAYMENTS.md](PAYMENTS.md) → "Lebanon launch".
- ✅ **Platform**: 4-role auth (demo + Auth.js), EN/AR i18n + RTL, notifications (inbox + email/SMS/push/WhatsApp seams), activity feed with `ACTION_CODES`, cron engines, E2E + prisma chain tests, `db:smoke`.

**Still-open boundaries documented in the existing docs** (these are the first places to look for "what's next"): live payments (P0), the rest of the W2 repo seam, review-moderation queue, OAuth linking, and the whole mobile plan.

## 2. Workflow enhancements — make the journeys better

Improvements to how customers, workers, and admins *move through* the product. Ordered by leverage.

### 2.1 Discovery phase (customer picks on better signals)

- [x] **Response rate + "Free this week" on search cards & profiles** — ✅ shipped (W1). `responseRate` (from `computeResponseRate` / shared `responseRateFromCounts`) and `availableThisWeek` (from `hasFreeSlotsThisWeek`, a 7-day AVAILABLE-slot window) are stamped on the `Worker` type by both adapters — demo via `withDemoSignals` in `repo.ts`, prisma via the batched `stampWorkerSignals` in `prisma-repo.ts` — and rendered as chips on `WorkerCard` + the `ProfileHero` meta row (i18n keys `worker.responseRate` / `worker.freeThisWeek` EN+AR). Unit tests + a `db:smoke` section assert the stamps against the live DB. *Why: customers select on responsiveness and availability, not just rating.*
- [ ] **💡 Price benchmarks per category + city** — a "typical quote for a leaking sink in Riyadh: SAR X–Y" line on profiles/search, computed from accepted quotes (`Booking.quote` where status in confirmed/inProgress/completed). *Why: anchors expectations and cuts quote-shock declines at the response phase.* Small.
- [ ] **🟡 Verified-purchase-weighted reviews** — `verifiedPurchase` exists on `Review` but isn't enforced or weighted; fold it into the P0 review-moderation queue (approve/reject, flag spam). Medium.

### 2.2 Request phase (fix the structural weakness)

- [x] **Multi-candidate "request quotes from up to 3 workers" — ✅ shipped per [multi-candidate-quotes.md](multi-candidate-quotes.md)** — a customer posts ONE job and invites up to `MAX_QUOTE_WORKERS` (3) workers to quote it; each responds with a bid (the existing quote/deposit inputs); the customer picks a winner + a slot from the winner's availability. A new `QuoteRequest` job container (`QR-YYYY-NNNNN`, `QuoteStatus` OPEN/QUOTING/SELECTED/EXPIRED/CANCELLED, `QUOTE_SLA_MS` 48h expiry) groups the invited workers, who ARE its slot-less `Booking` rows (`QUOTING`/`QUOTED` — `Booking.startAt/endAt` became nullable, migration `20260814080745_multi_candidate_quotes`). The winner's pick reuses the EXACT atomic `AVAILABLE → RESERVED` CAS + overlap guard (the winner becomes a normal slot-bound REQUESTED booking and flows through the existing respond pipeline; losers are system-DECLINED — slot-less, nothing to free; the job flips to SELECTED once). Both adapters (demo + `prisma.$transaction`) implement the same rules; the requests cron also expires quote jobs (`/api/cron/requests` → `quotesExpired`). UI: a "Get quotes" dialog on every worker profile (pick up to 3 from the profile + related pool), a **Quote requests** tab on `/bookings` (one card per job: bids per worker, "Accept this quote" → the winner's slot picker → `selectQuoteAction`), and the worker dashboard Requests tab renders QUOTING invites as an inline bid form + QUOTED as "Awaiting the customer's decision". i18n `booking.quotes*` + `booking.status.quoting/quoted` EN+AR; unit tests in `tests/bookings.test.ts` (create/duplicate/over-limit, bid, pick + guards, SLA expiry, actions round-trip), mapper tests in `tests/prisma-repo.test.ts`, and a `db:smoke` W2 section (create → 2 bids → pick → loser DECLINED + slot claimed → respond → CONFIRMED · backdated expiry → EXPIRED + bids DECLINED, idempotent). The customer-side list lookup matches by the signed-in `customerId` as well as email/phone — the quote form's email is optional, so a customer who skips it still sees their own jobs (both adapters + the /bookings page; unit-tested in `tests/bookings.test.ts` and asserted in the smoke's quote section with the email stripped). **The single highest-leverage workflow change — converts a lottery into a marketplace.** Large (but reused the Booking rails — no new payments logic).
- [x] **Request SLA (nudge + auto-expire) — ✅ shipped** — a `REQUESTED` booking that sits unanswered is dead air for both sides. The `runRequestSlaEngine` cron (`GET /api/cron/requests`, CRON_SECRET-gated like the other cron jobs) nudges the worker once past `BOOKING_SLA_NUDGE_HOURS` (24h) — `worker-request-nudge` notification, idempotent via the `Booking.lastSlaNudgeAt` CAS stamp (migration `20260813130000_request_sla`) — and auto-cancels past `BOOKING_SLA_EXPIRE_HOURS` (48h): status → CANCELLED (`cancelledBy` system), the slot frees back to AVAILABLE (rule 3), a SYSTEM audit event is appended, the customer gets `customer-request-expired` (deep-link `/bookings`), and `BOOKING_CANCELLED` is logged to the activity feed so the funnel's cancelled bucket and Recent activity tell one story. Both adapters return the same `RequestSlaRun` shape (demo dedupes nudges per process; prisma stamps the column). The two windows are tunable constants shared by both adapters. i18n `notifications.types.bookingRequestNudge/Expired` EN+AR; unit tests in `tests/request-sla.test.ts` (pure window boundaries + demo lifecycle: nudge-only, expire frees the slot + notifies, mixed batch, idempotent re-run) and a `db:smoke` W2 section (backdated request → nudge + auto-expire → slot freed → both notifications → feed entry → re-run expires/nudges nothing, with self-heal + cleanup). ALL THREE sides now render the same live ticking clock as a `BookingSlaCountdown` (compact on the rows): the admin dispute view's banner, the customer /bookings rows and the worker dashboard cards each show the real minutes ticking down against `requestSlaExpiryMs` plus the urgency bar (>50% green, 20–50% amber, <20% red pulse) — the worker's copy swaps to the nudge variant once the cron nudged. The quote cards' closing window now drains with the SAME shared `SlaUrgencyBar` (extracted from the countdown so the two deadlines can never drift): a live ticking "Closes in Nh Nm" line against the real `QUOTE_SLA_MS` deadline the cron enforces. Everything time-derived is made hydration-safe by the shared `useSsrSafeNow` hook: each server page passes its own render-time clock down as a `nowSeed` prop and the components render from it until mount (then the countdowns tick via `tick: true` — including the quote card's closing window), so the SSR markup and the client's first render are identical — no "A tree hydrated but…" mismatch on any of the three pages. The e2e hydration-smoke matrix (`tests/e2e-smoke.test.ts`) now guards the whole surface: `/admin/bookings/BK-1001` (the deep link whose banner used to mismatch on every load), `/dashboard`, `/bookings` (customer rows) and `/search`, EN + AR, asserting a zero-error browser console — and, beyond the console guard, CONTENT-checks the compact SLA countdown itself (`expectSlaBar`): the localized "Request auto-expiry" progressbar must render with a numeric `aria-valuenow` plus the ticking countdown copy on `/dashboard` and `/bookings`, so a silent regression that stops the countdown from rendering fails the matrix even with a clean console. *Why: less dead air, fewer stale slots — the worker is reminded while there's still time, and the customer gets a definitive answer instead of silence.*
- [ ] **💡 Guest phone OTP** — guests are phone-keyed with no verification; a one-time OTP raises trust enough that workers stop declining "anonymous" requests, and it de-risks M3 deposits for guests. Medium.

### 2.3 Response & negotiation phase

- [x] **Customer ⇄ worker chat thread — ✅ shipped** — a per-booking negotiation thread keyed on `Booking.id` (the `BookingMessage` model, migration `20260815090000_booking_chat`), so quotes are no longer one-shot and negotiation happens **inside the booking's record** instead of WhatsApp. The sender is actor-stamped like an audit entry (`senderRole` customer/worker/admin + optional real user id), and **quote sharing** lets the worker attach a price to a message (minor units, rendered as an in-thread quote chip). Both adapters implement the same rules: `getBookingMessages` (oldest-first read) and `sendBookingMessage` (append, unknown-booking → null) via `getBookingById` — the permission-gated `sendBookingMessageAction` (the same ownership gate as the audit-email action: the booking's customer, its worker, or a platform admin; guests can't send) validates text + the optional quote (major → minor ×100) and revalidates all three surfaces. One **shared `BookingChat` component** renders the identical thread on all three sides — the customer `/bookings` row, the worker dashboard booking rows (with the quote-sharing composer) and the **admin dispute view** (read-only, so the audit trail carries the whole conversation) — with a **WhatsApp deep-link fallback** (`buildWhatsappChatLink` in booking-ui.ts: `wa.me/<digits>?text=<prefilled booking context>`, targeting the other party's number — the worker's WhatsApp for the customer, the customer's phone for the worker). i18n `booking.chat*` EN+AR; unit tests `tests/booking-chat.test.ts` (demo adapter: ordering, actor stamping, quote share, unknown-booking) + `tests/booking-chat-action.test.ts` (permission gate, validation, quote conversion) + `tests/booking-chat.test.tsx` (thread rendering, quote chip, WhatsApp link, composer per role, EN+AR) + the `toDomainBookingMessage` mapper test; `db:smoke` exercises the thread on a live booking. Every send also **appends a `message` audit event to the booking's trail** (the `MESSAGE` status, migration `20260815093000_booking_chat_audit`, actor = sender, reason = message body) — the demo adapter pushes it inline and `prismaSendBookingMessage` wraps message + event in one transaction, so negotiations surface in the dispute timeline like every other action, with `booking.status.message` i18n + badge/funnel keys. **The customer can accept the worker's quoted price straight from the thread** — `acceptChatQuoteAction` (customer-ownership gate, same as the audit-email action) calls `acceptChatQuote` on both adapters: the REQUESTED booking converts to CONFIRMED with the message's quote (minor units), the slot is booked, the M5 take-rate fee is stamped from the worker's current plan, and a CUSTOMER `confirmed` audit event lands in the trail (dispute view shows the negotiation → agreement). The accept button renders only on the customer's side, only on a worker's quoted message, and only while the booking is still requested; the worker gets a `worker-quote-accepted` notification. **Read receipts + typing indicators**: `BookingMessage.readAt` (migration `20260815100000_booking_chat_read_receipts`) stamps the counterpart's messages when a party opens the thread (`markChatRead` on both adapters, idempotent — already-stamped rows untouched), so each side sees a **Seen** badge on their own messages; typing is ephemeral and **shared** via a small `chat-presence.ts` module (TTL, set/clear, reset with the demo store) merged by the `getChatPresence` seam into a snapshot (typing role + readAt map) that the client polls (`setChatTypingAction` fires on composer input, cleared on send/unmount). Mark-read + typing run through `markChatReadAction`/`setChatTypingAction`/`getChatPresenceAction`, all party-gated (the booking's customer or worker only — admins read-only). *Why: the negotiation stays inside the booking's audit trail — visible in the dispute view — instead of leaking to WhatsApp.*
- [x] **Customer confirms completion — ✅ shipped** — workers no longer self-mark COMPLETED. A worker's "Mark complete" flip is **staged**: the booking lands in a new `completionPending` status (`COMPLETION_PENDING` + the `Booking.completionPendingAt` stamp, migration `20260813140000_completion_pending`) with a `worker` audit event, and the customer is pushed a "Confirm job completion" notification (deep-link `/bookings`, app type `bookingCompletionPending`). The customer's **Confirm** (`confirmCompletionAction` → seam `confirmBookingCompletion` → demo/prisma adapters, CAS on the status inside `$transaction`) flips it to COMPLETED with a `customer` audit event — and THAT is where the payout ledger credit lands (`creditEarningsInTx`, idempotent) and the worker is told "Customer confirmed completion — your payout is on its way" (`worker-completion-confirmed`). If the customer never confirms, the grace cron (`GET /api/cron/completions`, CRON_SECRET-gated, engine `runCompletionAutoConfirmEngine` in `src/lib/data/completion-auto-confirm.ts`) auto-confirms staged completions past `BOOKING_COMPLETION_CONFIRM_GRACE_HOURS` (72h, tunable constant) with a `system` audit event, credits the ledger and emails the customer the completion receipt — idempotent via the CAS (overlapping cron runs can't double-flip). The funnel gains a `completionPending` bucket (accepted-side for conversion), the admin funnel card + `BookingStatusBadge` + worker Past tab ("Awaiting confirmation") render it, and the customer /bookings row shows the violet Confirm card. `customerEmailKind` treats customer-confirmed completions like customer cancels (no self-email; the worker got it). Unit tests in `tests/completion-confirmation.test.ts` (grace boundary, staged-not-completed, confirm credits + notifies, non-staged reject, auto-confirm + idempotency) + updated lifecycle/payout/funnel/bucket tests; `db:smoke` M4 ops now walks staged → customer-confirmed, plus a new M4 §2.3 section proving the auto-confirm engine live (backdated stamp → COMPLETED + receipt + ledger, re-run no-op). *Why: the most credible trust fix — fake-COMPLETED noise can no longer pollute the funnel, ratings, and no-show stats, and the customer's word gates the payout.*
