## Full Description

**WorkersArena** is the leading marketplace for finding trusted professionals in Lebanon and the MENA region.

🔹 **For Customers:**
- Browse 20+ categories: plumbers, electricians, carpenters, AC technicians, and more
- Read verified reviews from real customers
- Compare profiles, prices, and availability
- Book directly through the app with secure payments
- Get instant quotes from multiple professionals

🔹 **For Workers:**
- Get qualified leads delivered to your phone
- Manage bookings, payments, and scheduling in one place
- Grow your business with a professional profile
- Accept payments via OMT, Whish, or card
- Track your earnings and ROI

🔹 **Features:**
- 30-day free trial for new workers
- Category-adjusted pricing (fair rates for every trade)
- Lead marketplace with bronze/silver/gold grades
- Push notifications for bookings and reminders
- Offline support for spotty connections
- Bilingual: Arabic RTL + English LTR

Join 2,400+ professionals and thousands of customers who trust WorkersArena.
